Project of the Year at DPFA 2025

On 15 November, the main design event of the year, DPFA 2025, came to an end, with the Kraster Wallet hardware cold crypto wallet triumphing and becoming the most talked-about nominee of the evening, taking home the statuettes for best project in the categories Interfaces, Product Design, UI Design, and finally — Project of the Year.

It was a busy day, with the release of Kraster Wallet selling out to nearly a thousand people.

We believe that this result would not have been so impressive if the counterparties had purchased edits and interfered in the work process instead of demonstrating competence, as is predictably not the case.

We are pleased that we were once again able to confirm this not with words, but with deeds, and we express our gratitude to KTS Limited for their trust and responsible work on the technical specifications and other product documentation at all stages of production, of which there were many.
Vlad Goncharov
Head of Design

Kraster Wallet is a new hardware cold cryptocurrency wallet that allows you to store and manage digital assets using a card and your phone.
